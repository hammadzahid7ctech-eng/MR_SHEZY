Our Story

Welcome to Mr. Shezy Pizza Shop

At Mr. Shezy Pizza Shop, located in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi, we are passionate about serving quality food crafted to satisfy every craving. From our signature handmade pizzas loaded with rich cheese and premium toppings to our delicious selection of burgers, pasta, wings, and sides, every item on our menu is designed to deliver a memorable dining experience.

Whether you are exploring new flavors or sticking to your all-time favorites, our menu offers a wide variety of choices for everyone. Browse our selection, discover your next favorite meal, and feel free to reach out to us with any enquiries.
